FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SafeReach.

What is SafeReach?

SafeReach is a community safety platform that connects people with emergency alerts, reports and essential safety resources.

How do I report an incident?

Select the Report option from the navigation menu, choose the incident category and provide the required information.

Can I receive emergency alerts?

Yes. SafeReach provides real-time alerts to help keep users informed about important safety events.

Are emergency resources verified?

SafeReach is designed to provide reliable emergency information and verified resources.
