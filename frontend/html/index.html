Frequently Asked Questions

Is SafeReach free for residents?

Yes, the core SafeReach platform is completely free for individual residents to receive alerts and report incidents.

Does SafeAlert replace 911?

No. In an immediate life-threatening emergency, always call 911. SafeAlert is designed for situational awareness and community communication.

How is information verified?

Reports undergo a multi-layered verification process involving community coordinators, public safety officials, and cross-referencing with other verified sources.
