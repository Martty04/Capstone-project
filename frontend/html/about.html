The SafeReach Story

SafeReach didn't start in a boardroom; it started in a basement following a city-wide blackout. When official channels went silent, the community relied on fragmented, unverified social media posts. The chaos that followed proved that people don't just need information—they need truth.

Our founders, a team of emergency response coordinators and software architects, envisioned a civic safety tool that combined the speed of social media with the authority of emergency services.

Today, SafetyAlert serves as the bridge between public concern and official action, providing a stable, reliable platform for real- time reporting and community support.
